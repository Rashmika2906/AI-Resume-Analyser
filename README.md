# AI Resume Analyser
A smart resume analysis tool that helps you understand exactly why you're not getting shortlisted and what to fix before your next application.

## Why I Built This 
Most people apply to jobs without knowing if their resume even matches the job description. This tool gives you an honest ATS score,points out what's missing , and even predicts the interview questions you'll likely face -all based on your resume and the specific job you are applying for.

## What it Does
-Uploads your resume as PDF
-ATS Score - see how well your resume matches the job
-Missing Keywords - know exactly what recruiters are looking for 
-Strengths - see what's already working in your resume
-Interview Questions - prepare for questions likely to come from your resume
-Suggestions - actionable improvements to make
-Analysis History - track your progress compare old vs new scores

## What Makes this Different 
Most resume tools just give you a score and forget you. This tool saves your previous analyses so you can actually compare - did your score improve after fixing your resume? Which areas got better? You can track your own growth session by session.

## Tech Stack
-Python
-Gemini AI(LLM)
-Langchain
-Streamlit
-PyPDF2

## How to Run Locally
1.Clone this Repository.
2.Install Dependencies.
3.Create a `.env` file and add your Gemini API key.
4.Run the app.

This tool uses the free tier of Gemini API. If you hit a quota limit, wait a few minutes and try again.
